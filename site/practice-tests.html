Practice MCQ Tests

The same pre-test and post-tests used in live cohorts, made interactive: pick a test, answer each question, and get marked right or wrong immediately — with the correct answer, why, and which module it comes from, whenever you miss one.

7 tests: 1 Pre-Test + 5 Module-wise Tests + 1 final Post-TestFormat: instant per-question feedback, no login, nothing saved

Take the Pre-Test before Day 1 to see where you're starting from — it's fine if most of it feels unfamiliar. Each Module-wise Test covers exactly one day's material, for practice right after that day. The Post-Test is the final, holistic one, drawing from all 6 days.
